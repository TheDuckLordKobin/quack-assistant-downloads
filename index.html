Quack Assistant // installation sequence

Set up your command deck.

Install in five steps

01
Download the APK

Tap the download button below. Your browser may ask where to save it.

02
Allow this browser to install apps

When Android blocks the file, tap Settings and turn on “Allow from this source” for the browser you used.

03
Install and open Quack Assistant

Tap Install, then Open. The app will guide you through your name, preferred voice, and activation word.

04
Choose your permissions

Allow the features you want; they are explained on the right. You can change them later in Android settings.

05
Choose your assistant

Pick your voice and HUD, then say your activation word to start your first conversation.

Recommended settings

◉
MicrophoneNeeded for voice commands and your custom wake word.
⌁
Battery: UnrestrictedAndroid Settings → Apps → Quack Assistant → Battery. This helps it stay ready when the screen is off.
⌁
NotificationsOptional. Turn this on if you want to ask Quack Assistant about notifications.
⌖
LocationOptional. Needed for local weather and directions.
◌
Notification accessOptional. Android Settings → Special app access → Notification access. Only enable it if you want notification summaries.

The install is about 217 MB because it includes offline voice options. Keep your phone on Wi‑Fi while it downloads.
